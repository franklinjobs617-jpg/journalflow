## New Beginnings

1. What word do you want to define this year? Why that word?
2. What are you leaving behind from last year — intentionally?
3. What does a good year look like for you, specifically?
4. What's one thing you want to feel more of this year?
5. What's one thing you want to feel less of?
6. What would you do this year if you knew you couldn't fail?
7. What habit would change your life if you built it this January?
8. What relationship do you want to invest more in this year?
9. What do you want to learn this year?
10. What does your ideal version of December 31st look like?

## Reflection

11. What was the best thing that happened last year?
12. What was the hardest thing you navigated? What did it teach you?
13. Where did you grow in ways you didn't expect?
14. What's something you're proud of from last year that you haven't given yourself credit for?
15. What didn't go the way you wanted — and what, honestly, is that telling you?
16. What relationships changed last year? For better or for harder?
17. What belief did you update about yourself or the world?
18. What did you try that didn't work, and are you glad you tried?
19. What did your life teach you last year that you're carrying into this one?
20. Who do you want to thank for something they did for you last year?

## January Intentions

21. What does a successful January look like for you?
22. What's one thing you'll do this month that Future You will thank you for?
23. What's something small you can change in January that would have a big impact?
24. What does rest look like this month — real, intentional rest?
25. Who do you want to show up as in January?
26. What's one creative goal for this month?
27. What's a January ritual you want to create?
28. What are you willing to say no to this month to protect your energy?
29. What's one act of courage you'll take in January?
30. How will you take care of your mental health this month?

## Winter Reflection

31. How does January feel in your body — what does this season ask of you?
32. What does January energy feel like compared to other months?
33. What do you love about winter, if anything?
34. What's hard about this time of year for you?
35. Write about the quiet of January. What does it make possible?
36. What does your ideal winter day look like?
37. What would it mean to embrace the slowness of January rather than fight it?
38. What does the new year mean to you — is it hopeful, overwhelming, neutral?
39. What light — literal or metaphorical — are you looking for this month?
40. Write about renewal. What does starting fresh actually feel like for you?

## Deeper Intentions

41. What do you want your life to feel like, day to day, this year?
42. What's a dream you're committing to this year, even if you're scared?
43. What's something you're releasing from last year that you've been holding too tightly?
44. What does "showing up for yourself" look like this year?
45. What's one thing you'd tell your January self at the end of this year?
46. What values do you want guiding your decisions this year?
47. What does your intuition say about this year?
48. What are you most hopeful about?
49. What's one relationship you want to deepen this year?
50. Write a letter to yourself to open on December 31st. What do you want to say?
