## This Month's Energy

1. What does November mean to you — what feeling or memory does this month carry?
2. What do you want this month to be about?
3. What's one intention you're setting for November?
4. What do you want to feel more of this month?
5. What would a really good November look like for you?
6. What's something about this time of year that you love?
7. What's something about this season that's challenging for you?
8. What does your body need this month?
9. What does your heart need this month?
10. What's one small change that would make November better than last November?

## Reflection

11. What happened last month that you're still carrying?
12. What are you proud of from the past few weeks?
13. What's one thing you've learned recently about yourself?
14. What relationship has meant the most to you lately?
15. What's a moment from the last month you want to remember?
16. What did you start that you want to keep going?
17. What did you try to force that maybe needs a different approach?
18. What's something good that happened that you haven't fully appreciated?
19. What's something you want to do differently this month?
20. Where are you right now — honestly — in your life?

## Daily Life This Month

21. What does a good day look like for you this month?
22. What's a routine or habit that's serving you right now?
23. What's something in your daily life that's draining you?
24. What would your ideal morning look like this month?
25. How do you want to wind down at the end of the day?
26. What are you reading, watching, or listening to that's feeding you?
27. What's something beautiful you've noticed this month?
28. Who are you spending the most time with? Is that where you want to be putting your energy?
29. What's one thing you want to do this month that's just for you?
30. What does rest look like for you right now?

## Goals & Growth

31. What's the most important thing you're working on this month?
32. What's one thing you'll do this month that you'll thank yourself for later?
33. What are you learning right now — formally or informally?
34. What's a fear you want to move toward this month?
35. What's a goal you've been putting off that could start this month?
36. What does "making progress" look like for you right now?
37. What would you do this month if you stopped waiting to feel ready?
38. What support do you need that you haven't asked for?
39. What's something you want to create or build this month?
40. What does showing up for yourself look like this November?

## Deeper Questions

41. What's something you've been meaning to say to someone that you keep putting off?
42. What does this season ask of you that you've been resisting?
43. What's a question you're sitting with this month?
44. What part of your life feels most alive right now?
45. What part of your life feels most stuck?
46. What would it mean to trust yourself a little more this month?
47. What's something you're hoping for — something real, not a fantasy?
48. What's one thing you can let go of before this month is over?
49. Write about where you are on your journey right now. Not where you want to be — where you actually are.
50. What do you want to remember about this month when it's over?
