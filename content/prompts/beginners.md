The blank page doesn't have to be scary. These 100 journal prompts for beginners are designed to be your way in  -  questions simple enough to answer in five minutes, interesting enough to keep you writing for thirty. No journaling experience required. No right or wrong answers. Just you and a page, figuring out what you think and how you feel. Start with whichever one you like. Come back tomorrow. That's the whole practice.

## Journal Prompts for Beginners: Simple Starters

1. How are you, really? Not the version you give when someone asks in passing  -  the real answer.
2. Describe your day in three words. Then explain why you chose those three.
3. What's something good that happened today, even if everything else was hard?
4. What's on your mind right now? Just write it out  -  whatever's there.
5. What did you eat today and how did it make you feel? (This is a surprisingly good starting point.)
6. Describe where you are right now  -  what you can see, hear, smell.
7. What's one thing you're looking forward to, even something small?
8. What's something you did today that you're glad you did?
9. Who did you talk to today? What did you talk about?
10. What's one thing you wish had gone differently today?
11. Write one sentence about how this morning felt.
12. What's something that made you smile recently?
13. What do you need right now  -  rest, connection, space, movement?
14. Describe one thing you noticed today that you might normally overlook.
15. What's a question you've been thinking about lately, even if you don't have an answer?
16. What's something you're grateful for today? Write one specific thing, not a list.
17. How does your body feel right now? Tired, tense, relaxed, energized?
18. What's something you're proud of from this week, even something small?
19. What's something you want to remember about today?
20. Write about one thing that's been on your mind and you haven't said out loud yet.
21. What did you spend most of your energy on today? Was it worth it?
22. What's one thing you learned today  -  about anything?
23. Describe the best part of your day in as much detail as you can.
24. What's something you want to do more of?
25. If today had a color, what would it be? Why?

## Beginner Journal Prompts for Self-Discovery

26. What are three things you actually like about yourself?
27. What's something you're good at that you don't give yourself enough credit for?
28. What kind of environment makes you feel most like yourself?
29. What's a small thing that makes your day noticeably better?
30. What do you do to recharge when you're drained?
31. What's a topic you could talk about for hours without getting bored?
32. What's something you do just for yourself, not for anyone else?
33. What does a good day look like for you? Not a perfect day  -  just a good one.
34. What's something you're curious about right now?
35. What's a simple pleasure you enjoy that you don't talk about much?
36. What's something you always make time for, no matter what?
37. When do you feel most at ease?
38. What's something you've changed your mind about in the last year?
39. What's one thing you want more of in your life right now?
40. What's one thing you want less of?
41. What's something you find genuinely interesting that most people don't?
42. What's a skill you wish you had? What would you use it for?
43. What's something you'd do more of if you had more time?
44. What's a value  -  something you care about  -  that shows up in how you live?
45. What does "having a good week" look like for you?
46. Who makes you feel like yourself when you're around them?
47. What's something you've been meaning to try but haven't yet?
48. What's a word that describes how you want to feel more often?
49. What does comfort look like to you?
50. What's something small you could do today that would make tomorrow a little easier?

## Easy Journal Prompts for Weekly Reflection

51. What went well this week? Don't overthink it  -  just what comes to mind.
52. What's something you handled better than you expected recently?
53. What's one thing you want to do differently next week?
54. What's something someone did for you recently that you appreciated?
55. What's been taking up the most space in your head lately?
56. What's something that felt hard at first but got easier?
57. What's a moment from the past month you want to remember?
58. What's something you're still figuring out? It's okay not to have an answer.
59. What's one thing you've been avoiding? Just name it  -  you don't have to solve it today.
60. What made this week different from last week?
61. What's something you did this week that took more courage than it looked?
62. Who or what helped you get through a hard moment recently?
63. What's something you did this week just because you wanted to, not because you had to?
64. What's something you're learning about yourself lately?
65. What's a small win from this week that you haven't celebrated?
66. What's something you're carrying right now that you could put down, even temporarily?
67. What would make next week feel successful?
68. What's one thing you did today that was kind  -  to yourself or someone else?
69. What's something you're looking forward to this week, even something ordinary?
70. What's one thing that's been better lately than you expected?

## Journal Prompts for Beginners: Your Life Right Now

71. Describe where you are in life right now in 3-5 sentences. Just the basics.
72. What's something that's changed in your life in the last year?
73. What does a typical weekday look like for you?
74. What's a relationship in your life that you value? What do you value about it?
75. What's something in your life that's working well right now?
76. What's something in your daily routine that you actually enjoy?
77. What's something you're working toward right now, big or small?
78. What's something about your life right now that you want to remember?
79. What's one thing that's been surprisingly okay lately?
80. What does your ideal morning look like? How close is your actual morning to that?
81. What's something you've figured out recently  -  about yourself, your life, anything?
82. What's something you appreciate about where you live?
83. What's a book, show, podcast, or song that's been part of your life lately?
84. What's something you've been doing for yourself lately?
85. What's a small, good thing that's been a constant in your life?
86. What's something about your life right now that you don't want to take for granted?
87. Who are the people you spend the most time with? What do you like about them?
88. What's something you're glad you started  -  a habit, a hobby, a relationship?
89. What's a part of your daily life that brings you a small, quiet joy?
90. What would you want someone to know about your life right now  -  the real version, not the highlight reel?

## Beginner Journaling Prompts When You Have No Idea What to Write

91. Write about what's in your line of sight right now. Describe three things you can see.
92. What did you have for your last meal? Did you enjoy it?
93. Write one true sentence. Then another. Then another.
94. What's the last thing that made you laugh?
95. Write down three things that happened today in the order they happened.
96. What's something you're thinking about that has nothing to do with work or responsibilities?
97. What's the weather like right now, and how does it match or not match how you feel?
98. Write about something you noticed today  -  anything at all.
99. What's something you'd like to say to someone right now that you probably won't?
100. Write one thing you want to remember about today, and one thing you'd like to leave behind.
